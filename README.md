# Training Platform Website

A web-based Training Management Platform designed to connect organizations with students who are interested in professional training programs, practical learning, and skill development.

The platform allows organizations to share information about their activities, publish training opportunities, manage student applications, and showcase completed training programs through photos and updates.

## 🎯 About the Platform

The Training Platform provides a centralized website where students can discover available training programs and apply online.

The organization manages the entire training process through an admin dashboard, from publishing training opportunities and reviewing applications to documenting completed training programs.

## ✨ Main Features

### 🏢 Organization Information
- Display organization name, logo, and profile.
- About the organization section.
- Organization mission and vision.
- Organization goals and activities.
- Contact information and location.
- Display services and programs offered by the organization.

### 🎓 Training Programs
- Publish available training programs.
- Display training titles and descriptions.
- Training objectives and learning outcomes.
- Training duration and schedules.
- Training location.
- Application opening and closing dates.
- Display training requirements and eligibility.

### 👨‍🎓 Student Registration
- Student account registration and login.
- Personal student profiles.
- Submit training applications online.
- Provide required personal and educational information.
- Upload supporting documents when required.
- View application status.
- Receive application approval or rejection updates.

### 🔐 Admin Dashboard
- Secure admin login.
- Manage student accounts.
- View all submitted applications.
- Review student information.
- Approve or reject applications.
- Manage training programs.
- Manage training participants.
- Update training schedules and information.
- Publish completed training activities.
- Upload and manage training photos.
- Manage website content and organization information.

### ✅ Application Approval Process

The platform follows a structured application process:

1. The organization publishes a training opportunity.
2. Students register on the website.
3. Students select a training program and submit applications.
4. The admin reviews each application.
5. The admin approves or rejects applications.
6. Approved students participate in the training.
7. After the training is completed, the organization publishes photos and training results.

### 📸 Completed Training Gallery
- Display completed training programs.
- Upload training photos.
- Organize photos by training program.
- Display training titles and descriptions.
- Show training dates and locations.
- Share highlights of training activities.
- Showcase the organization's achievements and impact.

### 📢 News & Announcements
- Publish organization announcements.
- Share upcoming training opportunities.
- Display important updates.
- Share completed activities and events.

## 🔄 How the Platform Works

**Student Workflow**

1. Visit the website.
2. Read about the organization.
3. Browse available training programs.
4. Create an account.
5. Apply for a training program.
6. Wait for admin approval.
7. Check application status.
8. Attend the approved training.

**Admin Workflow**

1. Log in to the admin dashboard.
2. Create and publish training programs.
3. Receive student applications.
4. Review student details.
5. Approve or reject applications.
6. Manage approved participants.
7. Complete the training program.
8. Upload training photos and publish the completed training gallery.
